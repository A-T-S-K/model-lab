# Unfamiliar-user teaching check

**HUMAN TEACHING VALIDATION: PENDING**

Full unfamiliar-user testing follows independent M5 foundation acceptance; M6 station and release acceptance remain separate. See the [foundation ledger](foundation-status.md). This is a concise future protocol for the current Guided exhibit, not a claim of validated learning.

Give an unfamiliar participant the running Guided page without a technical explanation. Ask them to make a prediction, follow Part 1 and Part 2, inspect one number more deeply, and decide what to do with the candidate. Observe unprompted actions first; offer assistance only after recording where it was needed.

Ask in the participant's own words:

1. What was the model predicting, and what was the known target? Point to one authentic forward number and explain the operation that produced it.
2. What is the difference between an attention score and an attention weight? Is either the next-character output probability? Why is a future causal Key absent?
3. Show one retained gradient contribution and the running partial. How do both differ from the completed final gradient?
4. What does the final gradient tell us? What does Adam propose, and has the accepted parameter changed yet?
5. What does the baseline/candidate comparison establish on this one example? What would Accept or Discard do? Does this prove general improvement?
6. Open Values, Exact Math, Source, or a scalar Microscope where available. Can you return to Guided or resume after Explore without losing the computation you were following?

Record date, exact build/runtime identity, relevant prior experience without identifying details, unassisted words and actions, assistance, errors, and one concrete improvement idea. Distinguish observed numbers from derived calculations, verified recomputation, structural relationships, interpretation, and unavailable evidence. Missing is not zero; replay is not fresh execution; explanation movement is not runtime timing. Keep participant findings separate from browser qualification and owner review.
