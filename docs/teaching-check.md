**HUMAN TEACHING VALIDATION: PENDING**

Give an unfamiliar visitor the running Guided page without a technical introduction. Ask them to make a prediction, teach the model, and investigate one changed number. Observe where they pause or need help; do not explain the answer first.

Afterward ask:

- What was the model trying to do? What did its probability mean?
- What is a parameter? What changed when you taught the model, and why?
- What did attention do?
- Could you find the code responsible for one operation?

Record the date, build/runtime revision, participant's relevant prior experience (without identifying details), their own words, where assistance was needed, and one concrete change to try next. Separate unassisted understanding from facilitator explanations. Repeat with unfamiliar people after changes. Browser tests and reviewer opinions do not establish comprehension.
